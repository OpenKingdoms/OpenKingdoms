OpenKingdoms

An open-source engine for Total Annihilation: Kingdoms

OpenKingdoms ships the engine only. To play, point it at a copy of the game you own. Pick your Total Annihilation Kingdoms folder, which brings the music and your maps along too, or the archives inside it.
