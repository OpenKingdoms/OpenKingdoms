OpenKingdoms

An open-source engine for Total Annihilation: Kingdoms

OpenKingdoms ships the engine only. To play, point it at a copy of the game you own. Pick your Total Annihilation Kingdoms folder, or the .hpi files inside it.

Drop your game folder or its .hpi files here

Your files never leave your machine. The page reads them locally and, if you tick the box, keeps a copy in this browser's private storage. Nothing is uploaded. More about game files.
